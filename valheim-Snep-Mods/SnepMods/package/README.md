# SnepMods

A small pack of gameplay tweaks. Press **Shift+P** in-game to open the settings
menu — pick a mod from the list to see its own settings page.

## Mods included
Few QoL mods that i'm making for me and my friends and thought about shareing them. (will be adding more in the future)

### AutoShield
Equip a one-handed weapon and your last-equipped shield comes with it
automatically — no more re-clicking your shield every time you switch weapons.
- Toggle key: **F7** (rebindable in the menu)

### Compass
A heading strip at the top of the screen showing cardinal directions and
nearby map pins (death markers, bosses, your placed pins, other players, etc.)
projected by direction.
- Toggle key: **F8** (rebindable in the menu)
- Adjustable width and height
- Each marker type (death, boss, campfire, home, hammer, circle, portal,
  other players) can be shown or hidden individually

### AreaRepair
Repair a piece with your hammer and any other damaged pieces within a radius
of it get repaired too — no need to click each one individually.
- Toggle key: **F9** (rebindable in the menu)
- Adjustable radius (1-20 meters)
- Respects privately-warded areas, same as vanilla repair

### FloatingItems
Items you drop (or that fall) into water float on the surface instead of
sinking to the bottom.
- Toggle key: **F10** (rebindable in the menu)
- Adjustable buoyancy force
- Only applies to items dropped after the mod is active — items already
  resting underwater won't retroactively float

## Settings menu

Open with **Shift+P**. Click a mod's name to see its settings. While rebinding
a key, press **Escape** to clear it instead of assigning Escape as the key.

## Compatibility

Client-side only — no server-side requirement.
